What the Roster does

Every capability in the app, grouped by what it's for rather than which screen it's on. For step-by-step instructions, see the How-To Guide; for a guided tour, see the Walkthrough.

Officer builds and manages the roster Staff checks it and manages their own day Both either role uses this

Building the roster

Turning a blank week into a fully staffed one.

Calendar coverage mapOfficer

Four weeks at a glance, colour-coded green/orange/red/grey. Short on-call slots are called out with a letter code even on an otherwise red day.

Week TemplatesOfficer

Apply a saved coverage skeleton to any week in one tap. Only ever fills gaps — never overwrites what's already on the roster.

Day view assignmentOfficer

Build the day activity by activity, with Consultant/Registrar dropdowns ranked by who's done that activity least.

Duty AssignmentsOfficer

A configurable set of on-call slots (First On Call, Obs On Call, and however many more a department needs) alongside the day's activity cards.

Fortnight viewOfficer

Assign person-first instead of slot-first, with an automatic supervising-consultant fallback when slotting in a junior doctor.

Excel roster import & exportOfficer

Bring an externally built roster in from Excel, or export the department's roster back out — the same code mapping, both directions.

Keeping it fair & safe

The rules that stop the same few people carrying every hard shift.

Advanced SkillsOfficer

Tag staff with skills like Anaesthetics, Obstetrics, or Endoscopy, then require one on an Activity or Duty Type — staff missing it don't appear in that slot's picker at all.

Staff Activity ProfilesBoth

Staff flag activities they can't or won't do; officers still see and can pick them, just clearly marked.

Shift Pattern RulesOfficer

Configurable fatigue rules — like mandatory rest after a night shift — enforced with a required override reason whenever one has to be broken.

On-Call & Weekend Fairness ReportOfficer

Tallies on-call and weekend load per person, so it stays visibly even across the department.

Case Mix ReportOfficer

Tracks each staff member's exposure by activity — the same ranking that powers the Consultant/Registrar dropdowns.

Staff self-service

Everything a staff member needs without calling the office.

Day & Week viewsStaff

Today's allocation front and centre, plus the whole week at a glance, one card per day.

Phone BookStaff

The week's on-call roster and every other department number, all tap-to-call.

VariationStaff

Volunteer for an unfilled shift, or notify sick with one tap — an officer approves either straight from Day view.

CoffeeStaff

One tap texts the whole department's coffee order, totalled by drink and milk, to the coffee place.

Availability calendarStaff

Mark available/unavailable day by day, including standing days off, fortnightly patterns, and blocks of leave.

Install as an appBoth

Adds to the home screen on iOS or Android like a native app — no app store needed.

Two-factor authenticationBoth

Optional, per person: add an authenticator-app code to sign-in on top of your password, toggled on or off any time in Settings → Security.

Officer administration

The configuration behind everything above.

Staff AccountsOfficer

Invite staff by email, set officer vs staff permission, and correct rank.

Payroll ExportOfficer

Download any fortnight's whole-department roster as an Excel file, ready for pay processing.

ApprovalsOfficer

Sick reports and shift-volunteer requests both surface in Day view for a one-tap approve or deny.

Reference dataOfficer

Shifts, Locations, Activities, Duty Types, Leave Types, and Phone Book entries — every list the rest of the app draws from, editable in Settings.
