Guided tour

A Week on the Roster

One realistic pass through the app, start to finish — from an officer opening the week to a staff member texting the coffee order. Follow it start to end, or use it to show someone else how the pieces fit together.

Officer step Staff step
Setting up the week
1
Officer

Open the Calendar and check coverage

Four weeks are on screen at once, coloured by how well each day is covered. A run of red or orange days is where to start.

Green = fully covered · Orange = on-call short · Red = a required activity has nobody on it.

2
Officer

Apply the Week Template

Pick the standard template from the dropdown above the grid and hit Apply on the week that needs it. This creates the empty cards for everything that has to happen — nobody's assigned yet, but the Calendar can now correctly flag what's missing.

Once only

A template only needs applying once per week. Skip this step entirely if the roster's already built by hand for that week.

3
Officer

Fill in Day view

Tap a red day to jump into it. For each card, open the Consultant and Registrar dropdowns — names are ranked by who's done that activity least, so working down the list keeps experience spread fairly.

If last week already looked like this one, Copy Last Week's Activities (top of Day view) brings across the same cards empty — quicker than building from a Week Template when there isn't one for this week.

Some slots are locked down

If an activity has a required Advanced Skill set (e.g. Endoscopy needing Anaesthetics or Endoscopy), only staff holding that skill appear in its dropdown at all — set who holds what under Settings → Staff & Contacts → Staff and Availability, and what each activity requires under Settings → Locations & Activities.

4
Officer

Set who's on call

In the Duty Assignments panel at the top of Day view, pick a name for each on-call slot — First On Call, Obs On Call, whatever your department runs. This is what turns an orange day green. A slot with a required Advanced Skill (e.g. Anaesthetics for an Anaesthetics on-call) only offers staff who hold it, the same as the activity cards above.

5
Officer

Slot in a junior doctor from Fortnight view

Switch to Fortnight, pick the junior from the staff list, and click their day. Choosing a shift shows everything already happening in that session — pick a card to join it as registrar. If nothing's there yet but someone's on call, you're offered them as supervising cover instead, and a fresh card gets built with both of them on it.

Through the week — staff side
6
Staff

Check today, see who's with you

The Day tab leads with today's own allocation, then Morning/Afternoon/Night sections below it. Tap the arrow on your allocation to see who else is at your location for that session.

7
Staff

Look up who's on call

The Phone Book tab lists the week's on-call roster by type. Tap a name to open their contact card, then tap the phone number to call directly — no need to look anything up elsewhere.

8
Staff

Pick up an unfilled shift

Something short-staffed shows up under Variation. Hit Volunteer to send a request.

9
Officer

Approve the request

Back in Day view, the volunteer shows up as a clickable name right inside that card's dropdown — pick them the same way you'd pick anyone else, and the request is resolved.

When plans change
10
Staff

Report sick

On the Variation tab, Notify Sick is enabled whenever there's a shift on today. One tap sends the report.

11
Officer

Approve it from Day view

A red banner appears at the top of Day view with the name and Approve / Deny buttons. Approving is what lets whoever's on call know cover is needed.

The small stuff
12
Staff

Sort the coffee order

Whoever's got the coffee run opens the Coffee button, checks the Order Summary at the top, and taps Text order to [Coffee Place] — it opens messages with the whole order typed out, ready to send.

13
Staff

Catch up with a friend, or check on a partner

Search for a colleague and star them from their card — they now show under the Week tab. Tap their name to see their week, or tap Crossover to see, at a glance, which days this week you're both rostered on and which you're both off.

That's the whole loop. For anything not covered here, the How-To Guide has the full reference, screen by screen.
